About Me

Although I have been in Springfield, MO for most of my life, I'm originally from the Chicago area. For the last several years I’ve been a CCMA at an orthopedic office that specializes in hand surgery, injuries, and wound care. Before that, I had been working in sterile pharmacy as a CPhT, compounding medications for intravenous infusion. Now, I'm pursing a computer science degree, pivoting from my ten year medical career to learn technology inside and out. Ultimately, my aspirations align with combining my experience with my studies and working with one of the companies currently at the front line of electronic health records and medication management.
